Segment 1 · French Revolution — 背景 + Three Estates 15min

一句话理解

The French Revolution (1789) was a political revolution in France. The French people overthrew the absolute monarchy and fought for liberty, equality, and fraternity.

法国人民推翻了君主专制，追求自由、平等、博爱。

核心概念

English中文Core meaning
Ancien Régime旧制度The social and political system in France before 1789
Absolute monarchy君主专制King has unlimited power — no constitution, no parliament to stop him
Estate等级French society was divided into three Estates（三个等级）
Constitutional monarchy君主立宪King's power is limited by a constitution（宪法）
Republic共和政体No monarchy; leaders are elected by the people

Three Estates（三个等级）— 极重要！常考！

Estate成员人口比例交税？权力
First EstateClergy（教士/神职人员） ~1% ❌ 不用交税 Very powerful — controlled education and beliefs
Second EstateNobility（贵族） ~2% ❌ 不用交税 Held top government and military positions
Third EstateCommoners（平民） ~97% ✅ 承担全部税负 Almost no political power

💡 记忆关键： 最富的两个等级不交税，最穷的97%人口交全部税。这就是革命的根本原因！

📖 翻书

OCR p.65-68（教材 p.64-67）—— 读 Causes of the Revolution 部分

✅ 快速自检

Q: What was the Ancien Régime?

A: The social and political system in France before the Revolution (1789).

Q: What were the Three Estates? Which one paid all the taxes?

A: First Estate (clergy), Second Estate (nobility), Third Estate (commoners). The Third Estate paid all the taxes.

Q: What is the difference between absolute monarchy and constitutional monarchy?

A: In an absolute monarchy, the king has unlimited power. In a constitutional monarchy, the king's power is limited by a constitution.

Segment 2 · French Revolution 的五大原因 15min

⚠️ 常考！DBQ（资料题）最爱考原因分析

#Cause中文详细解释
① Social inequality 社会不平等 Three Estates system was unfair. The 3rd Estate (97% of people) had no privileges but paid all taxes
② Financial crisis 财政危机 France was bankrupt from expensive wars — especially helping the American Revolution (支持美国独立战争)
③ Enlightenment ideas 启蒙思想 Ideas of liberty, equality, democracy (Locke, Rousseau, Montesquieu) inspired people to demand change
④ Economic hardship 经济困难 Bad harvests（歉收）→ food shortages → bread prices skyrocketed（暴涨）→ people were starving
⑤ Weak leadership 国王软弱 Louis XVI was indecisive（优柔寡断）. He failed to solve France's financial and social problems

📝 记忆口诀 — 5个关键词：

Inequality · Bankruptcy · Enlightenment · Hunger · Weak King

不平等 · 破产 · 启蒙思想 · 饥饿 · 国王软弱

📖 翻书

OCR p.65-71（教材 p.64-70）—— 每个原因的详细解释

✅ 快速自检

Q: List three causes of the French Revolution.

A: (1) Social inequality — the Three Estates system. (2) Financial crisis — France was bankrupt. (3) Enlightenment ideas — people were inspired by liberty and equality. (Also: economic hardship, weak leadership.)

Q: How did Enlightenment ideas contribute to the French Revolution?

A: Thinkers like Locke (natural rights), Rousseau (social contract), and Montesquieu (separation of powers) inspired the French people to demand liberty, equality, and democracy.

A: The Fall of the Bastille — the start of the French Revolution.

A: A period of violence (1793-94) led by Robespierre, during which thousands of people were executed by guillotine.

A: Napoleon came to power, ending the Revolution.

Segment 4 · French Revolution 的影响 10min

#Impact中文解释
① End of absolute monarchy 终结君主专制 France would never again have a king with unlimited power
② Spread of ideas: liberty, equality, democracy 传播自由、平等、民主理念 These ideas spread across Europe and inspired other revolutions
③ Rise of nationalism 民族主义兴起 People began to identify with their nation（民族/国家）, not just their king
④ Inspired later revolutions 启发后世革命 Other countries looked at France and demanded their own rights

Data-based Question 练习

📄 Source: "The Third Estate bore all the burdens of the state. The nobles enjoyed privileges without contributing anything. The Church owned vast lands but paid no taxes."

— Adapted from Abbé Sieyès, What is the Third Estate? (1789)

  1. According to the source, which group paid all the taxes?
  2. Which two groups paid no taxes?
  3. How does this source help explain the causes of the French Revolution? Use your own knowledge.
🔑 参考回答

1. The Third Estate paid all the taxes.

2. The First Estate (Clergy) and Second Estate (Nobility).

3. This source shows the social inequality in France before the Revolution. The Third Estate (97% of the population) paid all the taxes while the clergy and nobility paid nothing. This unfair system was one of the main causes of the French Revolution. Enlightenment ideas about equality and natural rights (e.g. from Locke and Rousseau) made people question why they should accept this inequality. When combined with a financial crisis and bad harvests, the Third Estate demanded change — leading to the Revolution of 1789.

Part B — 简答（用英文写完整句）

Scientific Revolution:

  1. What was the Scientific Revolution?
  2. How did Newton's Clockwork Universe Theory change Europeans' thinking?

Enlightenment:

  1. What is the social contract? Which thinker proposed it?
  2. Explain the separation of powers.

Industrial Revolution:

  1. Give three reasons why the Industrial Revolution started in Britain.
  2. What were two positive and two negative impacts of the Industrial Revolution?

French Revolution:

  1. What were the Three Estates?
  2. List three causes of the French Revolution.
  3. What was the Reign of Terror?

Cross-topic:

  1. How did the Enlightenment contribute to the French Revolution?
  2. Explain the connection between the Scientific Revolution and the Industrial Revolution.
🔑 Part B 参考回答

1. The Scientific Revolution was a major breakthrough in scientific theories in the 17th century, where Europeans shifted from Church authority to observation and experiment.

2. The Clockwork Universe Theory showed that the universe follows fixed laws that humans can discover through reason. This encouraged rational thinking and led to the Enlightenment.

3. The social contract is an agreement between the people and the government. If the government breaks it, people can overthrow it. It was proposed by Rousseau.

4. Separation of powers (Montesquieu) means dividing government into three branches: executive, legislative, and judicial. This prevents any one group from having too much power.

5. Britain had: (a) rich coal and iron, (b) a developed banking system, (c) a large colonial market, (d) a stable government, (e) advances in textile machinery.

6. Positive: Mass production made goods cheaper. Urbanization and the rise of the middle class. Negative: Child labour. Pollution from coal and factories.

7. The Three Estates were: First Estate (Clergy), Second Estate (Nobility), Third Estate (Commoners — 97% of the population).

8. Three causes: (1) Social inequality from the Three Estates system. (2) Financial crisis from expensive wars. (3) Enlightenment ideas about liberty and equality.

9. The Reign of Terror (1793-94) was a period of violence led by Robespierre, during which thousands of people were executed by guillotine.

10. Enlightenment thinkers proposed ideas like natural rights (Locke), social contract (Rousseau), and separation of powers (Montesquieu). These ideas inspired the French people to question absolute monarchy and demand liberty, equality, and democracy.

11. The Scientific Revolution developed scientific theories and a rational mindset. During the Industrial Revolution, these scientific theories were applied to practical production — for example, Newton's physics helped develop the steam engine. Science → technology → industry.
