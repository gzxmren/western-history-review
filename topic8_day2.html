Segment 1 · 经济发展 25min

Free Port（自由港）— declared in 1841. Ships could enter without paying harbour dues. This attracted foreign companies and made HK an international trading centre.

Entrepot Trade（转口贸易）— 核心经济支柱

HK became an entrepot（转口港）between China and the world market.

  • Imports: Indian cotton & opium, British manufactured goods
  • Exports: Chinese tea, silk, porcelain
  • Opium（鸦片） accounted for 86.5% of HK's total exports in 1847

Segment 2 · 社会状况 25min

Population Growth

10,000 (1841) → 370,000 (1898). Driven by mainland Chinese fleeing wars (Taiping Rebellion) and famine.

Bubonic Plague 1894（鼠疫）— 常考！

Broke out in Tai Ping Shan area. Government did Operation Tai Ping Tei — disinfection. But poor communication with Chinese caused panic. Chinese hid their illness → plague spread more.

Law and Order

ProblemGovernment measure

GamblingBanned in 1872

Segment 3 · 政策：歧视 → 笼络 → 教育 15min

Discriminatory Policies（歧视政策）

  • Chinese could not participate in politics
  • Double standard in court: Europeans lightly punished, Chinese faced harsh penalties (lashing)
  • European District Reservation Ordinance (1888) — segregated residential areas
  • Chinese banned from The Peak

Winning Over Chinese Elites（笼络华人精英）

  • Chinese merchants became rich from entrepot trade
  • Government needed their cooperation
  • 1880: Wu Tingfang（伍廷芳）became first Chinese unofficial LegCo member
  • Chinese elites became the bridge between government and Chinese community

Education Policy

  • Early years: government ignored education
  • 1848: first government schools
  • 1862: Central School（中央书院）— first government secondary school → later Queen's College（皇仁书院）
  • 1878: English became compulsory in all government schools

Segment 4 · 复习自测 10min

  1. What was a free port? Why was it important for HK?
  2. Name TWO industries that grew because of entrepot trade.
  3. What happened during the bubonic plague of 1894?
  4. Give TWO discriminatory measures against Chinese.
  5. When was the first Chinese appointed to LegCo? Who was he?
🔑 答案

1. A port where ships paid no harbour dues. It attracted merchants and made HK a trading centre.

2. Shipping / shipbuilding / banking / manufacturing (any 2).

3. Plague broke out in Tai Ping Shan area. Government disinfected but poor communication caused panic and resistance.

4. (1) Double standard in court — Chinese harshly punished. (2) European District Reservation Ordinance — segregated housing. (Or: Chinese couldn't live on The Peak, couldn't participate in politics.)

5. 1880, Wu Tingfang.
