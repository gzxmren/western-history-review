🏛️ Enlightenment 啟蒙運動

Enlightenment 啟蒙運動 — Intellectual movement 思想運動 applying rational thinking 理性思考 to society
Social contract 社會契約 (Rousseau) — People give up some rights for protection by government
Natural rights 天賦人權 (Locke) — Life, liberty, property 生命、自由、財產
Separation of powers 三權分立 (Montesquieu) — Legislative 立法, Executive 行政, Judicial 司法
Freedom of speech & religion 言論與宗教自由 (Voltaire)
Encyclopedia 百科全書 (Diderot) — Collected all human knowledge

🏭 Industrial Revolution 工業革命

Industrial Revolution 工業革命 — Shift from hand production to machines 從手工轉向機器生產
Steam engine 蒸汽機 (Watt) — Powered factories, trains, ships
Spinning jenny 珍妮紡織機 (Hargreaves) — Multiple threads at once
Railways 鐵路 (Stephenson) — Fast transport of goods & people
Urbanization 城市化 — People moved to cities for factory work
Child labour 童工 — Children worked in dangerous conditions

🔗 PART A — 事件背景分析題 Background Analysis ▶
💡 答題框架：因果鏈三步法
Step 1 — 定位事件 What
Step 2 — 往前找原因 Why：遠因(長期結構) → 近因(直接觸發) → 根本原因
Step 3 — 往後推影響 So What：直接影響 → 間接影響 → 長期影響
📝 Q1 — Background Analysis 背景分析
Explain why the Industrial Revolution began in Britain in the 18th century.
解釋為甚麼工業革命在18世紀始於英國。
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 Causal Chain 因果鏈
定位事件：Industrial Revolution began in Britain around 1760.

往前找原因：
① Agricultural Revolution 農業革命 — New farming methods (crop rotation, enclosures) increased food production → fewer farmers needed → surplus labour available for factories.
② Colonies & Trade 殖民與貿易 — Britain had colonies in America, India, and the Caribbean → supplied raw materials (cotton) and provided markets for British goods → huge profits to invest in machines.
③ Natural resources 自然資源 — Britain had abundant coal and iron ore → fuelled steam engines and built machines.
④ Political stability 政治穩定 — Britain had a stable government and strong property rights → encouraged businessmen to invest.
⑤ Key inventions 關鍵發明 — Watt's steam engine (1769), Hargreaves' spinning jenny, Stephenson's railways.
①農業革命釋放勞動力 ②殖民地提供原料和市場 ③煤炭鐵礦豐富 ④政治穩定保障投資 ⑤關鍵發明推動。
📝 Q2 — Background Analysis 背景分析
What were the causes of the French Revolution?
法國大革命爆發的原因是什麼？
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 Causal Chain 因果鏈
遠因 Long-term causes：
① Social inequality 社會不平等 — Ancien Régime divided society into Three Estates 三級制度. The Third Estate (97% of people) paid all taxes but had no political power. Clergy and nobles paid no taxes.
② Enlightenment ideas 啟蒙思想 — Thinkers like Rousseau (social contract), Locke (natural rights), Montesquieu (separation of powers) challenged the divine right of kings. People began to question authority.
③ Financial crisis 財政危機 — France helped America in the American Revolution → spent huge money → nearly bankrupt. King Louis XVI tried to tax the nobles but they refused.
④ American Revolution example 美國革命榜樣 — The success of the American Revolution (1776-83) showed that a people could overthrow a king and establish a republic.

近因 Immediate trigger：
⑤ Economic crisis 經濟危機 — 1788-89 crop failures → bread prices skyrocketed → widespread hunger and anger among common people.
⑥ Estates-General 三級會議 — King called the Estates-General (May 1789) for the first time in 175 years. The Third Estate demanded fair voting → King refused → they formed the National Assembly → Tennis Court Oath 網球場宣言 → King prepared to use force → Storming of the Bastille 攻佔巴士底獄 (14 July 1789).
📝 Q3 — Background Analysis 背景分析
How did the Enlightenment contribute to the outbreak of the French Revolution?
啟蒙運動如何促成了法國大革命的爆發？
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 Causal Chain 因果鏈
The Enlightenment provided the intellectual foundation for the French Revolution:

① Challenged divine right 挑戰君權神授 — Locke's theory of natural rights (life, liberty, property) argued that government's power comes from the consent of the governed, not from God. This directly undermined the legitimacy of absolute monarchy.
② Social contract 社會契約 — Rousseau argued that people enter a social contract with their government. If the government breaks this contract (by being unjust), the people have the right to overthrow it.
③ Separation of powers 三權分立 — Montesquieu's idea of separating legislative, executive, and judicial powers was a direct attack on the king's absolute power.
④ Spread of ideas 思想傳播 — Diderot's Encyclopedia spread Enlightenment ideas to educated commoners and the bourgeoisie. Salons and coffee houses became places where people debated politics.
⑤ American example 美國先例 — The American Declaration of Independence (1776) was heavily influenced by Enlightenment ideas — it proved that these ideas could be put into practice.
啟蒙運動提供了思想基礎：天賦人權挑戰君權神授、社會契約賦予人民反抗權、三權分立攻擊專制、百科全書傳播思想、美國革命證明可行。
📊 PART B — 對比分析題 Comparison ▶
💡 答題框架：對比三步法
Step 1 — 選維度 Dimensions：時間、原因、核心思想、手段、結果
Step 2 — 填內容 Data：在每個維度下列出A和B的對應內容
Step 3 — 下結論 Conclusion：指出最關鍵的差異或根本的相同點
📝 Q4 — Comparison 對比分析
Compare the ideas of Locke and Rousseau. What did they agree on? What was their main difference?
比較洛克和盧梭的思想。他們的共同點和主要分歧是什麼？
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 Comparison 對比框架
Dimension 維度John Locke 洛克Jean-Jacques Rousseau 盧梭
Core idea 核心思想 Natural rights 天賦人權: life, liberty, property 生命、自由、財產 Social contract 社會契約: people give up some rights for the general will 公共意志
Source of power 權力來源 Government's power comes from the consent of the governed 被統治者的同意 Sovereignty 主權 belongs to the people as a whole 全體人民
Right to rebel 反抗權 People can overthrow a government that violates natural rights 侵犯天賦人權 People can overthrow a government that ignores the general will 無視公共意志
Influence 影響 Influenced the American Revolution and the US Constitution Influenced the French Revolution (especially the radical phase)
結論 Conclusion：Both believed government's power comes from the people, not from God — this was revolutionary. Locke focused on individual rights (protecting property), while Rousseau emphasized the collective will of society.
📝 Q5 — Comparison 對比分析
Compare the living and working conditions of workers BEFORE and AFTER the Industrial Revolution.
比較工業革命前後工人生活和工作條件的變化。
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 Comparison 對比框架
Dimension 維度Before Industrial Rev 革命前After Industrial Rev 革命後
Workplace 工作場所 Home / cottage 家庭 — Domestic system 家庭手工業, worked at own pace Factory 工廠 — Worked in large buildings with machines, fixed hours 固定工時
Working hours 工時 Flexible, based on seasons and orders 12-16 hours/day, 6 days a week. Strict discipline — fined for being late
Wages 工資 Inconsistent, depended on orders Low wages, but regular pay. Women and children paid much less
Living conditions 居住 Lived in villages, fresh air, clean water Moved to cities → overcrowded slums 擁擠貧民窟, poor sanitation, disease spread fast (cholera, typhoid)
Child labour 童工 Children helped family at home, light tasks Children as young as 5 worked in factories/mines, dangerous conditions, long hours
Social structure 社會結構 Rural society, landowners & peasants New classes emerged: industrial bourgeoisie 工業資產階級 (rich factory owners) and industrial proletariat 工業無產階級 (factory workers)
結論 Conclusion：The Industrial Revolution brought higher productivity and more goods, but also harsh working conditions, urban poverty, and new social inequalities.
📝 Q6 — Comparison 對比分析
Compare the American Revolution (1776) and the French Revolution (1789). What similarities and differences can you identify?
比較美國獨立戰爭和法國大革命。有哪些相似和不同之處？
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 Comparison 對比框架
Dimension 維度American Revolution 美國French Revolution 法國
Cause 原因 Taxation without representation — colonies wanted independence from Britain Social inequality (Three Estates), financial crisis, Enlightenment ideas, crop failures
Enlightenment influence 啟蒙影響 Locke's natural rights — "life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness" Rousseau's social contract + Montesquieu's separation of powers
Nature 性質 War of independence 獨立戰爭 — colonies vs British Empire Internal revolution 內部革命 — people vs their own king
Outcome 結果 Stable republic 穩定共和國 — US Constitution still in use today Chaos → Reign of Terror → Napoleon's dictatorship → monarchy restored → eventually republic
Key document 關鍵文件 Declaration of Independence 獨立宣言 (1776) Declaration of the Rights of Man 人權宣言 (1789)
結論 Conclusion：Both were inspired by Enlightenment ideas and aimed to establish a government based on popular sovereignty 人民主權. However, America's revolution was against external rule (Britain) and led to a stable democracy, while France's was against internal tyranny (their own king) and led to instability before eventually achieving a republic.

📝 Q7 — Scenario: Source Analysis 史料場景分析
Study the following source and answer the questions below.
閱讀以下史料並回答問題。
📜 Source A — From the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (1789)
"Article 1. Men are born and remain free and equal in rights. Social distinctions may be based only on common utility.
Article 2. The aim of every political association is the preservation of the natural and imprescriptible rights of man. These rights are liberty, property, security, and resistance to oppression.
Article 3. The principle of all sovereignty rests essentially in the nation. No body nor individual may exercise any authority which does not proceed directly from the nation."
📝 Questions
(a) What Enlightenment ideas can you identify in this document? Quote specific phrases.
這段文獻中你能識別出哪些啟蒙思想？引用具體詞句。
(b) How does Article 3 challenge the traditional basis of royal power?
第3條如何挑戰了傳統的皇權基礎？
(c) Evaluate 評價： Despite declaring "all men are free and equal", women and slaves were excluded from these rights. Does this make the Declaration hypocritical? Explain your answer using the P-E-B-C framework.
儘管宣稱"人人自由平等"，婦女和奴隸卻被排除在外。這是否使宣言變得虛偽？用 P-E-B-C 框架解釋。
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 S-T-A-R + P-E-B-C
(a) Enlightenment ideas identified:
• Natural rights 天賦人權 (Locke) — Article 2 mentions "natural and imprescriptible rights" including liberty and property.
• Popular sovereignty 人民主權 (Rousseau) — Article 3 states "sovereignty rests essentially in the nation."
• Equality 平等 (Enlightenment ideal) — Article 1 declares "men are born and remain free and equal in rights."

(b) Challenge to royal power:
Article 3 states that sovereignty comes from the nation, not from God or the king. This directly contradicts the divine right of kings 君權神授 — the idea that the king's authority comes from God and cannot be questioned.

(c) Evaluation using P-E-B-C:
P — Position： I partially agree — the Declaration was both progressive and hypocritical.
E — Evidence for hypocrisy： Despite declaring universal rights, women could not vote or hold office. Olympe de Gouges wrote the Declaration of the Rights of Woman (1791) but was executed. Slavery in French colonies was not abolished.
B — Balance： However, we must view the document in its historical context. In the 18th century, the idea that all men are equal was already revolutionary. It laid the foundation for later movements for women's rights and abolition of slavery.
C — Conclusion： The Declaration was limited by its time, but its principles — liberty, equality, fraternity — became universal values that later generations fought to extend to everyone.
📝 Q8 — Scenario: Historical Situation 歷史場景
Imagine you are a factory worker in Manchester in 1830. Describe your daily life — your working conditions, living conditions, and how they compare to life before the Industrial Revolution.
想像你是1830年曼徹斯特的一名工廠工人。描述你的日常生活——工作條件、生活條件，以及與工業革命前生活的對比。
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 Scenario Analysis 場景分析
Working conditions 工作條件：
I work in a cotton factory from 6 am to 8 pm, 6 days a week. The factory is noisy, dusty, and dangerous — I've seen workers lose fingers to machines. There are no safety guards. If I'm late, I get fined. If I'm tired and slow down, I get beaten by the overseer. Children as young as 5 work here too, crawling under machines to clean them while they're still running.

Living conditions 居住條件：
I live in a small, overcrowded room in a damp basement. My family of six shares one room. There is no clean water or toilet — we share a privy with 20 other families. Disease spreads quickly; last year my younger sister died of cholera. The air is thick with coal smoke from the factories.

Compared to before 與革命前對比：
Before the Industrial Revolution, my family lived in a village. Life was poor but healthier — we had fresh air and clean water. We worked at home, at our own pace. Now we have regular wages but work longer and harder for less freedom. The factory owners get rich while we struggle to survive. However, there are more goods available in the shops — things like cotton cloth that were once luxuries are now affordable.

📝 Q9 — Essay 問答題
"The Industrial Revolution brought more harm than good to the working class." Do you agree? Explain your answer.
「工業革命帶給工人階級的害處多於好處。」你同意嗎？解釋你的答案。
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 P-E-B-C 評價框架
P — Position： I partially agree. The Industrial Revolution brought both significant benefits and serious drawbacks to the working class. Its overall impact depends on which aspects we prioritise.

E — Evidence of harm (agree)：
① Harsh working conditions 惡劣工作環境 — Workers worked 12-16 hours a day in dangerous factories. Accidents were common. Child labour was widespread.
② Urban poverty 城市貧困 — Rapid urbanization led to overcrowded slums with poor sanitation. Diseases like cholera killed thousands.
③ Loss of independence 失去獨立性 — Before, workers controlled their own time as cottage workers. In factories, they were disciplined by the clock and overseers.

B — Balance — Evidence of benefits：
① More jobs 更多就業 — Factories created mass employment. Despite low wages, workers had regular income.
② Cheaper goods 更便宜的商品 — Mass production made goods like cotton clothing, soap, and household items affordable for ordinary people.
③ Long-term improvements 長期改善 — Eventually, labour laws (e.g. Factory Acts) improved working conditions, trade unions gave workers a voice, and living standards rose.

C — Conclusion： In the short term, the Industrial Revolution caused immense suffering for the working class — harsh labour, urban slums, and child exploitation. However, in the long term, it also created the foundation for modern prosperity, higher living standards, and workers' rights. Whether it was "more harm than good" depends on whether we judge by the immediate experience of the first generation of factory workers or the long-term trajectory of human development.
📝 Q10 — Essay 問答題
"The French Revolution was a failure because it ended in Napoleon's dictatorship." Do you agree?
「法國大革命是失敗的，因為它最終以拿破崙的獨裁統治告終。」你同意嗎？
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 P-E-B-C 評價框架
P — Position： I disagree. Although the Revolution ended in Napoleon's dictatorship, its long-term achievements were significant and lasting.

E — Evidence that it seemed to fail：
① Ended in dictatorship 以獨裁告終 — Napoleon crowned himself emperor in 1804, betraying the Revolution's democratic ideals.
② Reign of Terror 恐怖統治 — 17,000 people were executed under Robespierre. The Revolution devoured its own children.
③ Monarchy restored 王朝復辟 — After Napoleon's defeat, the Bourbon monarchy was restored (1815-1830).

B — Balance — Evidence of lasting achievements：
① Abolition of feudalism 廢除封建制度 — The Revolution abolished the Three Estates, feudal privileges, and serfdom. Social equality became the norm.
② Declaration of the Rights of Man 人權宣言 — Its principles (liberty, equality, fraternity) became universal values that inspired movements worldwide.
③ Spreading revolutionary ideals 傳播革命思想 — Napoleon's wars spread revolutionary ideas (legal equality, nationalism) across Europe. These ideas could not be erased after 1815.
④ Long-term political impact 長期政治影響 — France eventually became a stable republic. The Revolution established the principle that sovereignty belongs to the people.

C — Conclusion： The French Revolution should be judged by its lasting principles, not by its short-term political instability. While it failed to establish a stable democracy immediately, it destroyed feudalism, established the principle of equality before the law, and spread democratic ideals across Europe and the world. In this sense, it was one of the most successful revolutions in history.
📝 Q11 — Essay 問答題
"The Enlightenment was more important than the Industrial Revolution in shaping the modern world." Do you agree?
「啟蒙運動比工業革命對塑造現代世界更重要。」你同意嗎？
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 P-E-B-C 評價框架 + Comparison 對比
P — Position： I partially agree, but I believe both were essential in different ways. The Enlightenment shaped our ideas and values, while the Industrial Revolution transformed our material life and economy.

E — Evidence for Enlightenment's importance：
① Foundation of modern democracy 現代民主的基礎 — Enlightenment ideas (natural rights, social contract, separation of powers) directly shaped the American Declaration of Independence, the French Declaration of the Rights of Man, and modern democratic constitutions worldwide.
② Human rights 人權觀念 — The concept of universal human rights, equality before the law, and freedom of speech and religion all come from the Enlightenment.
③ Secular thinking 世俗化思維 — The Enlightenment challenged church authority and promoted rational, scientific thinking that underpins modern education and science.

B — Balance — Evidence for the Industrial Revolution：
① Economic transformation 經濟變革 — The Industrial Revolution created the modern economy — factories, railways, mass production, global trade. This raised living standards dramatically over time.
② Technology & innovation 科技創新 — Steam power → electricity → modern technology. The Industrial Revolution set in motion a cycle of continuous technological innovation.
③ Social change 社會變革 — Created the working class → led to labour movements, democracy, and social welfare. Urbanization changed how people live.
④ Global impact 全球影響 — Industrialization enabled European global dominance and created the modern interconnected world economy.

C — Conclusion： The Enlightenment gave us the values (democracy, human rights, reason) and the Industrial Revolution gave us the means (technology, industry, global economy). Neither alone would be sufficient — the modern world is a product of both revolutions working together. I would argue they are equally important but in different spheres: one shaped our minds, the other shaped our material world.
⚡ PART E — 綜合挑戰 Mixed Challenge ▶
📝 Challenge Question — 跨主題綜合題
"The Enlightenment, the Industrial Revolution, and the French Revolution together transformed Europe from a feudal society into the modern world."

Explain how EACH of these three movements contributed to this transformation. In your answer, discuss:
① The specific changes each movement brought
② How the movements were connected to each other
③ Which movement you think had the greatest impact — and why

「啟蒙運動、工業革命和法國大革命共同將歐洲從封建社會轉變為現代世界。」
解釋這三場運動各自如何促成了這一轉變。在答案中討論：
① 每場運動帶來的具體變化
② 這些運動之間的相互聯繫
③ 你認為哪場運動影響最大——以及原因。
✅ Model Answer 答案示範 綜合答題
① The Enlightenment 啟蒙運動 — Changed ideas 改變思想
It challenged the traditional basis of authority (divine right of kings, church power) and replaced it with reason, individual rights, and popular sovereignty. It provided the intellectual foundation for democratic revolutions and modern political systems.

② The Industrial Revolution 工業革命 — Changed the economy 改變經濟
It replaced the feudal agricultural economy with industrial capitalism. New technologies (steam engine, railways) and the factory system transformed production, transportation, and daily life. It created new social classes (bourgeoisie and proletariat) and spurred urbanization.

③ The French Revolution 法國大革命 — Changed politics 改變政治
It destroyed feudalism in France, abolished the Three Estates, and established the principle of equality before the law. It spread revolutionary ideals across Europe through Napoleon's wars and inspired future revolutions.

④ How they were connected 相互聯繫：
The Enlightenment inspired both the Industrial Revolution (scientific thinking → technological innovation) and the French Revolution (ideas of liberty and equality → political revolution). The French Revolution created a more open society that allowed industrial capitalism to flourish. The Industrial Revolution created the wealth that funded Europe's global expansion.

⑤ Greatest impact — My view 我認為影響最大的是：
I believe the Enlightenment had the greatest impact because it caused the other two. Without Enlightenment ideas — rational thinking, questioning authority, belief in progress — neither the technological innovations of the Industrial Revolution nor the democratic ideals of the French Revolution would have been possible. The Enlightenment changed how people think, and once that changed, everything else followed.
