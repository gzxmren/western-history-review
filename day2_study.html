Segment 1 · 核心概念 15min

一句话理解

The Industrial Revolution was the shift from hand production to machine production in the mid-18th century.

工业革命是18世纪中叶从手工生产转向机器生产的变革。

五个关键术语

English中文Core meaning
Industrial Revolution工业革命 Mid-18th century — shift from hand-making to machine-making
Steam engine蒸汽机 James Watt's invention — the driving force behind the whole revolution
Factory system工厂制度 Workers come to a central place to work with machines, instead of working at home
Urbanization城市化 People moved from the countryside（农村）to cities（城市）to work in factories
Capitalism资本主义 Economic system based on private ownership and profit

💡 记住关键对比：

Before IR: People made things at home by hand（在家手工做）

After IR: People made things in factories with machines（在工厂用机器做）

✅ 快速自检

Q: What is the Industrial Revolution?

A: The shift from hand production to machine production starting in the mid-18th century.

Q: What is the factory system?

A: Workers come to a central place to work with machines, instead of working at home.

Q: What does "urbanization" mean?

A: People moving from the countryside to cities.

Segment 2 · Industrial Revolution 的五大原因 20min

#Cause（原因）中文Explanation
① Agricultural Revolution 农业革命 New farming methods → more food → population grew → more workers + more demand for goods
② Abundant natural resources 丰富的自然资源 Britain had lots of coal（煤炭）to power steam engines and iron（铁）to build machines
③ Colonial trade & capital 殖民贸易与资本积累 Colonies provided raw materials + markets. Merchants made huge profits and invested in machines
④ Scientific advancement 科学进步 Scientific Revolution → new knowledge → applied to practical production and technology
⑤ Stable government 稳定的政府 Britain had a stable political system that protected private property and encouraged business

Segment 3 · Why Britain?（为什么是英国？） 15min

⚠️ 常考！几乎每年必出 — "Why did the Industrial Revolution start in Britain?"

工业革命没有在法国、德国、意大利开始，而是在英国。记住这 五点：

Factor中文具体内容
🏭 Coal & Iron煤和铁 Britain had rich deposits of coal (to power steam engines) and iron (to build machines)
🏦 Banking system银行体系 Britain had a highly developed banking system. Rich people could invest money in new machines and factories
🌍 Colonial market殖民地市场 Britain had a large colonial empire. Colonies provided cheap raw materials AND bought British goods
🏛️ Stable politics政治稳定 Britain had a stable government that protected private property and supported business
🧵 Textile advances纺织技术进步 Britain already had advanced textile machinery (spinning jenny etc.), which gave it a head start

📝 记忆口诀 — 5个关键词：

Coal · Banks · Colonies · Stability · Textiles

煤 · 银行 · 殖民地 · 稳定 · 纺织

📖 翻书

OCR 找 "Why did the Industrial Revolution start in Britain?" p.36附近

✅ 快速自检

Q: Why did the Industrial Revolution start in Britain? Give at least 3 reasons.

A: (1) Rich coal and iron deposits. (2) A developed banking system. (3) A large colonial market. (Also: stable government, textile advances.)

Q: How did the colonial market help Britain's Industrial Revolution?

A: Colonies provided cheap raw materials (e.g. cotton) AND bought finished British goods, creating a huge market.

Segment 4 · 重要发明 15min

InventionInventor中文作用
Steam engine James Watt（瓦特） 蒸汽机 The driving force of the Industrial Revolution. Powered factories, railways, and ships
Spinning jenny Hargreaves（哈格里夫斯） 珍妮纺纱机 Spun thread much faster than by hand. Revolutionized the textile industry
Railways Stephenson（斯蒂芬森） 铁路 Allowed fast transport of goods and people. Connected factories to markets

💡 时代划分：The early Industrial Revolution (1760-1830) was called the "Steam Age"（蒸汽时代）. Steam power was everything!

工业革命的发展顺序

Textile industry（纺织业） was the first to change.

↓

Steam engine provided power → factories could be anywhere, not just near rivers

↓

Railways transformed transport → raw materials in, finished goods out

✅ 快速自检

Q: Who improved the steam engine? What was its importance?

A: James Watt. It was the driving force of the Industrial Revolution — it powered factories, railways, and ships.

Q: Which industry was the FIRST to change during the Industrial Revolution?

A: The textile industry（纺织业）.

Q: What did the spinning jenny do?

A: It spun thread much faster than hand spinning, revolutionizing textile production.

Segment 6 · Day 2 复习自测 20min

Part A — 填空题

  1. The Industrial Revolution started in the           century.
  2. It began in          .
  3.           improved the steam engine.
  4. The early Industrial Revolution is also called the           Age.
  5. The           industry was the FIRST to change.
  6. People moving from countryside to cities is called          .
  7. Working in a central place with machines is called the           system.
🔑 Part A 答案
  1. 18th (mid-18th century)
  2. Britain
  3. James Watt
  4. Steam
  5. textile
  6. urbanization
  7. factory

Part B — 简答（用英文写完整句）

  1. Give three causes of the Industrial Revolution.
  2. Why did the Industrial Revolution start in Britain? (give 3-4 reasons)
  3. What was the importance of Watt's steam engine?
  4. What were two positive and two negative impacts of the Industrial Revolution?
🔑 Part B 参考回答

1. Three causes: (a) The Agricultural Revolution increased food production and population. (b) Abundant natural resources like coal and iron in Britain. (c) Colonial trade provided capital for investment.

2. Britain had (a) rich coal and iron, (b) a developed banking system, (c) a large colonial market, (d) a stable political system, and (e) advances in textile machinery.

3. The steam engine provided power for factories, railways, and ships. It was the driving force of the Industrial Revolution.

4. Positive: (1) Mass production made goods cheaper. (2) Urbanization and the rise of the middle class. Negative: (1) Child labour. (2) Pollution from coal and factories.
