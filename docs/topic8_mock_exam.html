Section D — Data-based Questions (30 marks)

💡 S-T-A-R: Source · Tone · Argument · Reliability

Source A — Treaty of Nanjing (1842), Article III

"It being obviously necessary and desirable, that British Subjects should have some Port whereat they may careen and refit their Ships, His Majesty the Emperor of China cedes to Her Majesty the Queen of Great Britain, the Island of Hong Kong, to be possessed in perpetuity."

Source B — Hong Kong Trade Statistics, 1847

"Total value of exports from Hong Kong in 1847: $2,560,000, of which opium accounted for $2,214,400 (86.5%). The remainder consisted mainly of tea, silk, and cotton goods."

— Adapted from Hong Kong Blue Book, 1847

Source C — Report on the Bubonic Plague, 1894

"The Chinese population in Tai Ping Shan live in extremely crowded conditions. Many families of ten or more share a single room. There is no proper drainage, and the water supply is contaminated. The houses are dark and poorly ventilated. It is not surprising that disease spreads rapidly here."

— Report of the Sanitary Board, 1894

Source D — Tung Wah Hospital Founding Inscription (1870)

"This hospital is established through the generous donations of Chinese merchants and with a grant of land from the Government. It will provide free Chinese medical treatment to the poor, maintain a funeral home for the unclaimed dead, and offer education to orphaned children. The hospital will also serve as a channel for the Chinese community to communicate their views to the Government."
