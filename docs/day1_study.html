Segment 1 · Scientific Revolution — 背景 + 三大概念 15min

背景

在 Scientific Revolution（科学革命，17世纪）之前，欧洲人相信：

The Earth was the centre of the universe. The Church said so, so it must be true.

地球是宇宙中心，教会说了就是对的。

Scientific Revolution 打破了这一点：

From "the Church says so" → To "Let me observe, experiment, and prove it."

从"教会说了算" → "我自己观察、实验来证明"

三大关键概念

① Scientific method（科学方法）

A systematic way of discovering truth:

Observation（观察）→ Hypothesis（假说）→ Experiment（实验）→ Theory（理论）

② Laws of nature（自然法则）

自然界有固定规律。苹果往下掉不是随机的，而是受 law of universal gravitation（万有引力定律）支配。

③ Clockwork Universe Theory（大机械宇宙观）

Newton proposed: The universe is like a great clock. Every part works according to fixed laws. Man can discover these laws through reason（理性）.

牛顿提出：宇宙像一座大钟，每个部件都按固定规律运行，人可以通过理性发现这些规律。

Segment 4 · Enlightenment（启蒙运动）— 背景 + 核心概念 15min

一句话理解

Applying the Scientific Revolution's "rational thinking" to society and government.

把科学革命的"理性思维"用到社会和政治问题上。

逻辑链（必记！）

Scientific Revolution → Nature has laws（自然有规律）

↓

Enlightenment → Society also has laws! We can use reason to find them.（社会也有规律！可以用理性找出来）

↓

French Revolution → Let's apply these ideas to overthrow bad government.

核心概念（4个必记）

English中文Core meaning
Natural rights自然权利 Rights you are born with: life, liberty, property（生命、自由、财产）. (John Locke)
Social contract社会契约 An agreement between the people and the government. If the government breaks it, people can overthrow it. (Rousseau)
Separation of powers三权分立 Power should be divided: executive（行政）, legislative（立法）, judicial（司法）. They check each other. (Montesquieu)
Divine right of kings君权神授 The old idea that the king's authority comes from God — Enlightenment thinkers rejected this completely.
✅ 核心一问

Q: What's the connection between the Scientific Revolution and the Enlightenment?

A: The Scientific Revolution found laws of nature. Enlightenment thinkers asked: why can't we find laws of society too? So they applied rational thinking to society and government.

Thinker中文Core Idea一句话记

Part C — 简答

用英文写完整句：

  1. What was one cause of the Scientific Revolution?
  2. Why did the Enlightenment happen AFTER the Scientific Revolution?
  3. What is the Clockwork Universe Theory? Why was it important?
🔑 Part C 参考回答

1. One cause was the Voyages of Discovery, which proved that the Earth was round and challenged traditional theories.

2. The Scientific Revolution taught Europeans to use reason to find laws of nature. Enlightenment thinkers then applied this same rational thinking to society and government, asking: "What are the laws of society?"

3. The Clockwork Universe Theory was Newton's idea that the universe is like a clock — every change follows fixed laws. It was important because it showed that humans could discover the laws of nature through reason, which encouraged rational thinking and led to the Enlightenment.
