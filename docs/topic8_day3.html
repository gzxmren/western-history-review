Segment 1 · 本地华人组织 20min

Tung Wah Hospital（东华医院）— 最重要的华人组织

Background: Chinese couldn't afford Western medicine. Dying Chinese gathered at Kwong Fook I Tsz（广福义祠）.

Founded: 1870. Funded by Chinese leaders + government.

Services: Free Chinese medicine, burial, education, elderly care. Also raised funds for mainland disaster victims (received plaques from Emperor Guangxu).

Role: Became the main organization serving Chinese. Later → Tung Wah Group of Hospitals（东华三院）.

Po Leung Kuk（保良局）

Background: Abduction and trafficking of women & children (mui tsai 妹仔 system) was serious.

Founded: 1882.

Work: Prevented abduction, rescued victims, provided shelter, helped with jobs / marriage / return home.

Lok Sin Tong（九龙乐善堂）

Founded: 1880. Managed charitable funds. Set up free graveyard for unclaimed bodies.

District Watch Force（团防局）

Founded: 1866, funded by Chinese merchants. Maintained law & order, conducted census, advised government on Chinese affairs.

Segment 4 · 最终自测 15min

  1. When did British rule of HK begin? Which treaty?
  2. What was the role of the Governor?
  3. What is a free port? Why was it important?
  4. Name TWO industries that developed because of entrepot trade.
  5. What was the 1894 bubonic plague? Where did it break out?
  6. Give TWO discriminatory measures against Chinese.
  7. Who was the first Chinese appointed to LegCo? When?
  8. Name TWO services provided by Tung Wah Hospital.
  9. What was the purpose of Po Leung Kuk?
  10. Name ONE foreign religious organization and its contribution.
🔑 答案

1. 1842, Treaty of Nanjing.

2. Head of HK government, representative of British monarch, chaired ExCo & LegCo.

3. A port where ships paid no harbour dues. It attracted merchants and made HK a trading hub.

4. Shipping / shipbuilding / banking / manufacturing.

5. A plague in 1894 in Tai Ping Shan. Govt disinfected but poor communication caused panic.

6. Double standard in court / European District Reservation Ordinance / Chinese couldn't live on The Peak.

7. Wu Tingfang, 1880.

8. Free Chinese medicine, burial, education, elderly care, fundraised for disaster victims.

9. Prevented abduction and rescued women and children.

10. Canossian Daughters of Charity — orphanages & schools. Or: London Missionary Society — Ying Wa College. Or: James Legge — Board of Education.
