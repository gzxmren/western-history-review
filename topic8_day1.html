Segment 1 · 三大条约（香港如何成为殖民地）15min

Hong Kong became a British colony in three steps:

1842 — Treaty of Nanjing → cession of Hong Kong Island

1860 — Convention of Beijing → cession of Kowloon Peninsula

1898 — Convention Respecting an Extension of HK Territory → lease of New Territories (99 years)

Treaty / ConventionYearWhat happened
Treaty of Nanjing1842After First Opium War, HK Island ceded to Britain → start of British rule
Convention of Beijing1860After Second Opium War, Kowloon Peninsula (south of Boundary St) ceded
Convention Respecting an Extension of HK Territory1898New Territories leased for 99 years (until 1997). Britain now ruled all of HK

Q: When did British rule over Hong Kong begin?

A: 1842, with the Treaty of Nanjing after the First Opium War.

Q: How was the New Territories acquired?

A: By lease in 1898 for 99 years.

Segment 2 · 政府结构 20min

PositionRole
Governor（总督）Representative of British monarch; very powerful — head of government, chaired ExCo & LegCo, could pardon criminals, command troops in emergencies
Executive Council（行政局）Advised the Governor on policy-making and drafted bills. Members appointed by Governor.
Legislative Council（立法局）Examined laws and public spending. Established 1843. First meeting 1844. All members appointed (no election).
Supreme Court（最高法院）Highest court, established 1844. Independent judiciary. Jury system. Laid foundation for rule of law.

Letters Patent（英皇制诰）and Royal Instructions（皇室训令）— issued by Queen Victoria in 1843, defined the Governor's powers and the government structure.

Q: Who was the head of the Hong Kong government?

A: The Governor.

Q: What were the two councils that assisted the Governor?

A: The Executive Council (policy-making) and Legislative Council (law-making).

Q: When was the Supreme Court established?

A: 1844.

Segment 3 · 早期政府四大特点 15min

  1. Under British supervision — Hong Kong government had to obey the British Colonial Office. British gov could amend HK laws, control diplomatic & military affairs.
  2. Governor had great power — Governor had final decision on bills, could handle local affairs independently, had some judicial powers (pardoning).
  3. Appointment system — All positions (Governor, ExCo, LegCo members) were appointed, not elected.
  4. British-dominated — Most high-ranking officials and councillors were British. Chinese were in the minority.

Segment 4 · 复习自测 10min

用英文回答：

  1. Name the three treaties that established British rule in HK and their years.
  2. What was the role of the Governor?
  3. What were the Letters Patent and Royal Instructions?
  4. Give TWO features of early British government in HK.
  5. What was the significance of the Supreme Court?
🔑 答案

1. Treaty of Nanjing (1842) — HK Island. Convention of Beijing (1860) — Kowloon. Convention Respecting an Extension of HK Territory (1898) — New Territories (99yr lease).

2. Representative of British monarch, head of HK government, chaired ExCo & LegCo, had executive, legislative and some judicial powers.

3. Documents issued in 1843 by Queen Victoria that defined the Governor's powers and government structure.

4. (1) British supervision — HK gov obeyed British Colonial Office. (2) Governor had great power. (3) Appointment system. (4) British-dominated.

5. It established the independent judiciary and the rule of law in Hong Kong.
